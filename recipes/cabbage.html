Black Vinegar Cabbage

Description

Crunchy sauteed cabbage in a simple black vinegar sauce.

Steps

  • cabbage (~1/3 of a head, ~5-8 large leaves)
  • 2 eggs (+ shaoxing wine)
  • 1 medium-large carrot
  • seasonings 1: soy sauce, oyster sauce, white pepper, garlic (powder or diced), salt
  • seasonings 2: black vinegar, spicy pepper powder/flakes (to taste)
    • (aged black vinegar is extra strong and very good)
  1. hand tear cabbage leaves into generous bite-size chunks, cut carrots into slices (diagonal cuts are good)
  2. scramble eggs in a bowl with salt, white pepper, shaoxing wine
  3. preheat oil, scramble eggs till cooked, put back in bowl
  4. add oil, cook vegetables until done but still crunchy
  5. lower heat a bit, mix in eggs and all of seasonings 1
  6. turn off heat, add seasonings 2 and mix well
  7. serve with eggs
