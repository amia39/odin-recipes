Yaki-Udon (Stir-fried Udon Noodles)

Description

Savory stir-fried udon noodles.

Ingredients

  • frozen udon noodles
  • chunk of pork shoulder (sub with other protein)
  • marinade: soy sauce, white pepper, cornstarch, shaoxing wine, cooking oil, mirin
  • vegetables to preference: cabbage, carrot, onion, bok choy, peppers, etc.
  • green onion
  • seasonings
    • soy sauce
    • dark soy sauce (for color)
    • oyster sauce
    • garlic (powder or fresh)
    • white pepper
    • dashi
    • mirin
    • salt
    • red pepper flakes/powder (for spice, to taste)
  1. cut ingredients into thinly siced wide strips
  2. put sliced pork in bowl and marinate for ~5+ min to tenderize
  3. start boiling a pot of water for udon
    • on rolling boil, put udon in, take out as soon as it separates (~1 min)
  4. heat oil in pan, stir fry vegetables on high heat in batches
  5. preheat oil, stir fry onions in pot until golden brown and well softened
  6. heat oil in pan, stir fry meat on high heat until cooked
  7. turn heat to low-med, put vegetables back in pan, put udon in pan, throw in seasonings to taste
  8. mix well and serve
