Japanese Chicken Curry

Description

Rich Japanese-style chicken curry with vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2-3 chicken thighs
  • 1 large russet potato
  • 2 medium carrots
  • 1 onion
  • curry block (half (4 cubes) of an 8-cube rectangular package)
    • Java Curry (rich dark flavor)
    • Golden (neutral base)
    • Vermont (sweeter)
    • Kokumaro (sweeter, velvety texture)
  • seasonings to taste
    • sweet: oyster sauce, mirin, sugar
    • savory: dashi, soy sauce, salt
    • bitter/depth: chocolate, cacao powder, coffee powder
    • richness: peanut butter
    • other: black/white/red pepper, garlic, ginger, cumin, shaoxing wine
  • corn starch / flour (for thickening)
  1. peel and cut potato and carrot into bite-size chunks
  2. soak potato in bowl of water to remove starch (if desired)
  3. peel and cut onion into slices
  4. cut chicken into bite sized pieces
  5. preheat oil, stir fry onions in pot until golden brown and well softened
  6. put chicken into pot, add some soy sauce, black pepper, shaoxing wine, continue stir frying until chicken is mostly cooked
  7. put carrot and potato into pot, add water until barely covering ingredients
  8. cover and heat until rolling boil
  9. reduce heat to low-medium (simmering), add preferred seasonings and mix well
  10. cover and simmer, stirring occasionally, until you can poke through potato/carrot with a chopstick
  11. turn off heat, mix in curry blocks until dissolved
  12. if it's not thick enough, you can boil it a bit more or mix in a cornstarch/flour slurry
  13. serve over rice
